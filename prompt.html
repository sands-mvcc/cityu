Before vs. After: Anatomy of an Effective Prompt

Vague / Weak Prompt

Unpredictable Output
"Explain phishing to my staff."

Why it fails: The AI doesn't know the audience's technical literacy, the tone required, the length limit, or the format. You'll likely receive a generic Wikipedia-style summary.

Optimized Expert Prompt

High-Precision Output
Act as an Information Security Awareness Specialist. Write a 3-paragraph educational memo for non-technical community healthcare workers explaining how to recognize email phishing attacks. Focus on urgency cues, suspicious sender domains, and safe reporting procedures. Keep the tone encouraging and professional. Format with bullet points for key red flags.
